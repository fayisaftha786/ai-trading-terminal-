Automation controls

AUTO OFF
Waiting for first scan

Automatic PAPER entries start OFF. The owner must explicitly enable them after a recent complete scheduled scan confirms all configured markets, fresh same-provider quotes/books/candles and the risk gates. Once enabled, the Durable Object alarm monitors approximately every 90 seconds while the service and public providers are available. It permits one open position at a time and up to five completed BUY→SELL cycles per UTC day; five is a ceiling, not a target. Per-position notional is capped at $8 with $2 kept unallocated. Entry sizing uses a 60 bps/side paper-fee assumption, 10 bps/side slippage and observed spread; dynamic take-profit must clear estimated round-trip costs. Daily loss guard is 1% ($0.10 at starting equity), with a 0.75% soft pause. Watchdog recovery restores auto-entry only when the owner’s prior opt-in permission is still recorded. Owner emergency stop and drawdown halt never auto-resume. This is periodic paper monitoring, not tick-level real-time execution or a guaranteed uptime service. Live order execution remains hard-locked.
